Italian Cheat Sheet 🇮🇹

A handy guide for navigating Tuscany with style and confidence!

The Basics
Hello / Goodbye (Casual) Ciao! Say: "Chow"
Goodbye (Formal) Arrivederci! Say: "Ah-ree-vah-DAIR-chee"
Thank you Grazie! Say: "GRAHT-zee-eh"
You're welcome Prego! Say: "PRAY-go"
English, please? Inglese, per favore? Say: "In-GLAY-zay, pair fah-VOR-ay?"
Food & Drinks
Another glass of wine, please Un altro bicchiere di vino, per favore Say: "Oon AHL-tro bee-kee-AIR-ay dee VEE-no"
An espresso, please Un caffè, per favore Say: "Oon cahf-FAY, pair fah-VOR-ay"
Pasta La pasta Say: "Lah PAH-stah"
Pizza La pizza Say: "Lah PEET-sah"
Dessert Il dolce Say: "Eel DOHL-chay"
Getting Around
Where is the bathroom? Dov'è il bagno? Say: "Doh-VAY eel BAHN-yoh?"
How much does this cost? Quanto costa? Say: "KWAN-toh KOH-stah?"
Fun & Survival
I am lost, but the wine is good! Mi sono perso, ma il vino è buono! Say: "Mee SOH-noh PAIR-soh, mah eel VEE-noh eh BWO-noh"
I need more Gelato immediately! Ho bisogno di più gelato immediatamente! Say: "Oh bee-ZOHN-yoh dee pyoo jeh-LAH-toh"
Cheers! (To health!) Salute! / Cincin! Say: "Sah-LOO-tay" or "Chin-chin!"
