Pack & Prepare

Things to consider:
  • Ensure your passport does not expire within 6 months of your return date from Italy. If it does, please take the time to renew your passport.
  • The grounds are grass and pebble. The villas share grounds and all events will take place on the common grounds.
  • We recommend checking the compatibility of your hair tools with the voltage in Italy (as it is much higher and can cause damage to the tools). We can provide or purchase when in Italy if needed.
  • If cash is desired, using the local ATMs is what we recommend. Many and most places accept major credit and debit cards.
What to Pack
  • Passport
  • Wallet
  • Medications
  • Phone & chargers
  • Converters
  • Comfy walking shoes
  • Pajamas
  • Shawls / sweaters (as the temperature drops at night)
and most importantly... Your Awesome Selves!
