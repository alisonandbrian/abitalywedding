General Inquiries About Location / Stay

Where Are We Going?

All events will take place on the shared grounds of Villa Lenka & Borgo Bernardini.

The villas are located just south of Lucca Centrale in the Tuscany region of Italy.

Navigation Tip: On maps, search for 'Coselli Collection - Villa Lenka'. It is the easiest to find, and the grounds are connected & walkable.

Where Do We Stay?

  • • Bridal Party: Invited to stay on property at Borgo Bernardini.
  • • Mothers & Sisters: Invited to stay on property at Villa Lenka.
  • • The Palmers: Invited to stay in Camaiore.
  • • Family & Friends: Invited to stay at Albergo Villa Marta. All 15 rooms are being held for Wednesday, Oct 13 – Sunday, Oct 17. If you would like to stay here please let us know. If you are looking to extend your trip, we can put you in contact with the hotel concierge.

Hotel Info: 13-minute walk to/from the wedding location, includes a pool, group rate, and a wonderful concierge. We highly recommend Villa Marta for convenience, but feel free to explore other options if you'd like to stay in the city centre!

How Do We Get There?

  • 1. Pisa Airport (PSA): 30–35 mins away by car or taxi.
  • 2. Florence Airport (FLR): ~1.5 hours away via train to Lucca, plus a 10-min taxi.
  • 3. Train: Arrive at Lucca Centrale station + 10-min taxi.
  • 4. Bus: From Camaiore to Lucca + 15-min taxi.

Note: Uber can be used to order taxis from Lucca Centrale and the villas.
